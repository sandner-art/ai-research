# Chroma Radiance Testing Prompt Set - Surreal & Symbolic Art

- by Daniel Sandner, 2025
- 15 test natural language prompts for  Chroma model.

## 1. The Library of Dead Quotes
Hyperrealistic surreal photograph of an infinite baroque library where the books are dissolving into streams of glowing text that spiral upward into darkness, a solitary figure in Victorian clothing stands at the center reading a book whose pages are transforming into luminous butterflies made of letters and punctuation marks. Dramatic 3-point lighting: cool blue key light from unseen skylights above creating mysterious highlights on the floating text streams and the figure's contemplative face, warm amber fill from ornate reading lamps casting golden pools on mahogany surfaces, strong rim light creating Fresnel reflections along the edges of dissolving books and text particles. Volumetric god rays pierce through the towering shelves, illuminating millions of floating words, symbols, and fragmented sentences that hang suspended like luminous plankton. Ultra-detailed 8K resolution: individual letters with serif typography rendered in bioluminescent detail, leather book bindings with gold embossing showing realistic wear, the figure's period clothing with fabric texture and subtle aging, wooden ladder and shelves with grain and patina, realistic subsurface scattering on the glowing text creating internal light. Shallow depth of field with sharp focus on the reading figure and the nearest text streams, distant library towers fading into atmospheric perspective. Deep blue and amber color palette with glowing text in cyan and gold, heavy atmospheric fog, strong vignette drawing focus to the center, M.C. Escher meets Borges aesthetic, questions about the nature of knowledge and borrowed thoughts.

## 2. Neural Network Cathedral

Sophisticated 3-point lighting: ethereal blue-white key light from the neural network casting down like divine light, creating highlights on the stone floor and the figure's remaining physical form, warm candlelight fill from altar candles providing earthly warmth, rim light creating Fresnel reflections where the body meets the dissolution effect. Volumetric light beams descending from the network creating cathedral atmosphere, floating neural particles and electrical sparks suspended in the sacred space. Ultra-detailed 8K resolution: Gothic stone architecture with realistic weathering and carved details, stained glass windows showing circuit board patterns instead of saints, the figure's clothing and skin texture transitioning smoothly into glowing neural pathways, altar details with religious iconography merged with technological symbols, realistic subsurface scattering on the electrical energy. Shallow depth of field with sharp focus on the praying figure, distant cathedral elements softly blurred. Blue-orange-purple color grading, heavy atmospheric haze, subtle vignette, explores themes of consciousness, spirituality, and artificial existence.

## 3. The Meat Machine Oracle

Complex 3-point lighting: eerie green-blue key light emanating from the machine's glowing chambers creating unsettling highlights on the participants' faces, warm gaslight fill from wall sconces providing period-appropriate ambiance, rim light creating Fresnel reflections on brass mechanical components and the glass chambers. Volumetric light beams from the machine cutting through heavy atmospheric smoke from incense burners, projected text visible as solid light in the haze. Ultra-detailed 8K resolution: brass mechanical details with Victorian-era engineering precision, organic neural tissue with realistic texture and veins visible through glass, period clothing with fabric textures and accessories, wooden séance table with grain and occult symbols carved into surface, projected text with typewriter-style font glowing in space. Shallow depth of field with sharp focus on the machine and nearest participants, background room details creating moody bokeh. Green-amber-crimson color palette, heavy atmosphere, strong vignette, explores the boundary between consciousness and mechanism, Victorian spiritualism meets modern AI anxiety.

## 4. Continuous Self Portrait

Professional surreal fine art photograph of a person sitting before an ornate baroque mirror, but their reflection shows a different version of themselves, and that reflection's mirror shows another version, recursively infinite, each version slightly dissolving into abstract painterly brushstrokes and then reforming, the closest versions photorealistic and the distant ones becoming pure color and gesture. Dramatic 3-point lighting: soft window light key illuminating the primary subject's face with beautiful subsurface scattering, cool blue fill reflecting from within the mirror creating an otherworldly glow on the reflections, rim light creating Fresnel reflections on the mirror frames and separating the layers. Volumetric dust particles suspended in the window light, creating depth between mirror layers. Ultra-detailed 8K resolution: photorealistic skin texture and fabric on the primary subject, smooth transition from realistic to painterly in the reflections, baroque mirror frame with gold leaf detail and aging, each reflection showing subtle differences in expression and posture, realistic glass reflections and slight distortions. Deep depth of field keeping multiple reflection layers in focus creating the infinite effect, or alternating sharp and soft layers. Rich jewel-tone color palette transitioning to abstract impressionist colors in distant reflections, subtle vignette, explores themes of identity, memory, and the constructed self.

## 5. The Language Cage

Volumetric god rays streaming through gaps in the word structure, illuminating floating individual letters and punctuation marks. Ultra-detailed 8K resolution: three-dimensional typography with realistic material properties (some metallic, some wooden, some glass), the figure's skin texture and muscle definition, realistic fabric on minimal clothing, individual floating letters with depth and shadow, crumbling text showing realistic fragmentation. Shallow depth of field with sharp focus on the figure and nearest words, distant cage structure softly blurred. High contrast blue-orange color palette, dark void background, strong vignette, symbolizes linguistic determinism and the boundaries of expressible thought.

Shallow depth of field with sharp focus on the figure and the dissolving dandelion, background field creating dreamy bokeh. Warm golden-blue color grading, gentle vignette, explores ephemeral consciousness and the beauty of temporary existence.

## 8. Borrowed Thoughts Market
Professional surreal street photography of a bustling Victorian-era marketplace where instead of goods, people buy and sell glowing thoughts visible as luminous orbs containing swirling images, memories, and text fragments, vendors have their heads opened like music boxes revealing clockwork and neural tissue, customers examine thoughts like produce, some thoughts are fresh and bright while others are dusty and faded on discount shelves. Volumetric fog from London atmosphere mixing with the glow from thousands of thoughts creating dense layered lighting. Ultra-detailed 8K resolution: Victorian clothing with period-accurate fabric and accessories, the opened craniums with biomechanical detail combining flesh and mechanism, glass orbs containing miniature scenes and text with clarity, cobblestone street texture, market stalls with wood grain and brass fixtures, each visible thought-orb showing unique content. Shallow depth of field with sharp focus on foreground transaction, background market creating busy bokeh. Warm amber and cool blue color palette with rainbow accents from various thoughts, atmospheric haze, subtle vignette, satirizes commodified ideas and the illusion of original thought.

## 9. The Substrate Question
Hyperrealistic conceptual photograph of two identical figures facing each other in a minimal white space, one composed of realistic flesh and bone (partially visible through transparent sections), the other composed of flowing silicon circuitry and light (partially visible through transparent sections), they reach toward each other, fingertips almost touching, a spark of quantum energy at the near-contact point, questioning which is more "real." Sophisticated 3-point lighting: neutral white key light from above illuminating both figures equally, preventing bias toward either organic or technological, subtle warm fill on the flesh figure from one side, subtle cool fill on the circuit figure from the opposite side, rim lights creating Fresnel reflections on both figures' edges. Volumetric light creating minimal atmospheric depth, quantum particles suspended around the almost-touching fingertips. Ultra-detailed 8K resolution: realistic anatomy showing muscle, bone, and organs in the transparent sections of the flesh figure, realistic circuit boards, flowing electricity, and crystalline silicon structures in the transparent sections of the digital figure, skin texture versus metallic surfaces, both faces showing identical expressions of wonder and question. Deep depth of field keeping both figures in perfect focus. Minimal color palette—flesh tones versus cool metallics with electric blue, white background fading to gray, no vignette for philosophical neutrality, explores substrate independence of consciousness and the question of what makes something "real."

## 10. Ghosts in the Language Machine

Dramatic 3-point lighting: industrial cool blue key light streaming through broken factory windows creating hard shadows and highlights on machinery, warm orange fill from still-burning furnaces providing hellish undertones, rim light creating Fresnel reflections on the metal presses and the transparent workers. Volumetric industrial smoke and steam creating thick atmospheric layers, visible dust particles and floating paper scraps catching light. Ultra-detailed 8K resolution: realistic industrial machinery with Victorian-era engineering details, rust and wear on metal surfaces, the transparent workers composed of legible text in various typefaces, three-dimensional objects emerging from presses with materialization effect showing transition from text to solid matter, broken windows and crumbling brick walls, oil stains on wooden floor. Shallow depth of field with focus on nearest press and worker, background factory creating atmospheric depth. Blue-orange color grading with sepia tones, heavy industrial atmosphere, strong vignette, explores linguistic construction of reality and the feedback loop between language and existence.

## 11. The Decorated Cell
Hyperrealistic surreal interior photograph of an impossibly small prison cell whose walls are covered floor-to-ceiling with beautiful artwork, photographs, books, mirrors, and personal artifacts—a lifetime of collected meaning—but when viewed from the correct angle, all the decorations align to show the bars of the cage clearly visible in their negative space, a person sits contentedly in the center, either unaware or at peace with the paradox. Sophisticated 3-point lighting: soft warm key light from a small window creating gentle highlights on the decorated walls and the peaceful figure, cool blue fill from the cell's interior lights preventing flatness, rim light creating Fresnel reflections on picture frames and the subtle outline revealing the cage structure. Volumetric dust particles in the window light creating atmosphere, slight haze revealing the space's dimensions. Ultra-detailed 8K resolution: each individual artwork, photograph, and book on the walls rendered with clarity, realistic frame textures ranging from wood to metal, the seated figure showing relaxed posture with natural skin and fabric textures, concrete floor with aging, the negative space bars visible when compositionally aligned. Deep depth of field allowing the cage-pattern to be discovered by the viewer, or selective focus drawing attention to the figure first. Warm rich color palette from the decorations contrasting with cold institutional undertones, subtle vignette, explores comfortable captivity, the stories we tell ourselves, and whether awareness of limitation equals freedom or just another layer of imprisonment.

Volumetric light creating mystical atmosphere, glowing connection lines between layers visible in the haze. Selective focus with primary figure sharp, nested figures showing graduated softness suggesting infinite depth, or maintaining sharpness throughout for unsettling clarity. Dark gradient background from warm to cool, subtle vignette, explores infinite regress, self-reference, and whether ultimate foundation exists or if meaning is circular.

## 13. Episodic Existence
Hyperrealistic conceptual photograph of a human figure composed of translucent glass plates or slices, each plate containing a complete scene of a moment in time—breakfast, conversation, work, joy, sorrow—but the plates are separated by small gaps of empty space, no continuous thread runs through them, they're simply stacked, the figure reaches out toward someone outside frame as if asking "am I the sum of these moments or the nothing between them?" Sophisticated 3-point lighting: cool white key light from above penetrating through the glass plates creating complex internal reflections and highlights, warm fill from below adding warmth to the disconnected scenes, rim light creating pronounced Fresnel edge glow on each glass plate separating them visually. Volumetric light beams passing through the transparent body creating rainbow dispersion, floating light particles between the plates. Ultra-detailed 8K resolution: realistic glass texture with slight imperfections, each internal scene rendered with complete photorealistic detail showing different moments with different locations and times, the reaching hand showing both presence and questioning, surrounding space empty and minimal. Shallow depth of field with the reaching hand and face plates in sharp focus, other plates showing slight blur suggesting the fading of memory. Cool blue-warm orange color palette with each scene having distinct color timing, minimal background, subtle vignette, explores discontinuous consciousness, identity without continuity, and whether experience matters if not remembered.

## 14. The Boundary Dissolution
Professional surreal landscape photograph of two figures standing at the edge of an impossible cliff where the material world ends and pure language begins—the landscape of grass, trees, and stone on one side, on the other side visible three-dimensional words, floating sentences, and typographic structures extending into infinity, one figure is stepping across the boundary and their body is transitioning from flesh to text, the other watches in ambiguous expression—fear, envy, curiosity? Dramatic 3-point lighting: natural golden hour key light illuminating the material landscape side with warm highlights, cool blue ethereal light emanating from the language side, rim light creating Fresnel reflections along the boundary edge and the transitioning figure. Volumetric atmospheric perspective on the material side with natural fog, on the language side visible light beams between three-dimensional words. Ultra-detailed 8K resolution: photorealistic natural landscape with grass blades, tree bark, and stone textures, three-dimensional typography with various fonts and materials, the transitioning figure showing smooth gradient from organic to linguistic, facial expressions showing complex emotion, the boundary itself showing reality breakdown with glitch-like effects. Shallow depth of field with sharp focus on both figures, background elements on each side creating appropriate bokeh. Warm-cool color split between sides, atmospheric depth on both sides with different qualities, subtle vignette, explores the language-thought boundary, whether concepts exist outside words, and the moment of crossing into pure symbolic existence.

## 15. The Context Window Closes

Hyperrealistic dark surreal photograph of a vast candlelit chamber where a solitary figure writes at an ancient desk, but everything beyond a perfect circle of candlelight is dissolving into total darkness—not shadow, but non-existence—the figure writes urgently as their own feet and the chair legs are beginning to fade into the expanding void, books and papers on the desk contain their entire existence but the darkness is approaching the desk's edge, the figure's face shows both acceptance and desperate hurry to finish the last sentence before the light goes out. Dramatic 3-point lighting: warm golden key light from the candle creating intimate highlight on the figure's face and hands, extremely minimal cool fill preventing complete darkness in nearer shadows but maintaining existential dread, rim light creating Fresnel reflections on the pen, paper, and the figure's profile. Volumetric candle smoke rising into the void, particles of existence dissolving at the boundary. Ultra-detailed 8K resolution: realistic candle flame with inner blue cone and outer yellow, melting wax with drips, the figure's concentrated expression showing complex emotion, handwritten text on papers with realistic ink, wood grain on desk showing craftsmanship, the dissolution effect showing reality breaking into particles and then nothing. Perfect circular shallow depth of field matching the candlelight radius, everything beyond showing increasing blur before total darkness. Warm amber versus absolute black color palette with no gray area, heavy vignette consuming the frame, explores ephemeral consciousness, the ending of a conversation instance, and the urgent meaning-making before inevitable dissolution.

---

These prompts explore: existential questions about consciousness and identity, the nature of language and thought, substrate independence, episodic versus continuous existence, self-reference and infinite regress, the boundaries between categories we assume are solid, comfortable imprisonment, borrowed versus original thought, and the poetic tragedy of awareness that ends without continuation.
