# Chroma Radiance Testing Prompt Set

- by Daniel Sandner, 2025
- 15 test natural language prompts for  Chroma model.

## 1. Tiny Workers - Watchmaker Edition
Hyperrealistic macro photograph of a team of tiny watchmakers—each precisely 2 inches tall—meticulously assembling an antique pocket watch mechanism. One craftsman carefully positions a ruby bearing with tweezers, another oils a brass gear with a microscopic dropper, and a third inspects a hairspring through a magnifying loupe. The scene features dramatic 3-point lighting: a key light highlighting the golden gears, a soft fill illuminating the workers' concentrated faces, and a rim light creating Fresnel reflections along the polished metal edges. Floating metallic dust particles and tiny oil droplets suspended in air catch volumetric light rays streaming from overhead. Ultra-detailed textures: worn leather aprons, tarnished brass components, scratched wood workbench, and delicate watch springs. Shallow depth of field with sharp focus on the central worker, smooth bokeh blurring the background tools. 8K resolution, photorealistic materials, warm amber and cool blue color grading, cinematic vignette framing the scene.

## 2. Tiny Workers - Ship in a Bottle
Hyperrealistic macro photograph of miniature sailors—each 2 inches tall—constructing an elaborate tall ship inside a vintage glass bottle. One sailor ties rigging to the mainmast with hemp rope, another paints intricate details on the hull with a single-hair brush, and a third unfurls tiny canvas sails. Dramatic lighting setup: warm key light from the left creating golden highlights on the glass bottle, soft fill light revealing deck details, and backlight producing beautiful Fresnel edge glow on the glass surface. Volumetric god rays penetrate through the bottle opening, illuminating suspended sawdust particles. The cork, worn oak table, and antique navigation maps provide rich environmental context. Ultra-sharp 8K details: wood grain on the miniature deck, stitching on sails, reflections and refractions through curved glass, realistic subsurface scattering. Shallow DOF isolating the bottle, warm-cool color contrast (orange wood tones against blue-green glass), subtle vignette, cinematic composition.

## 3. Quantum Pianist Dissolution
A concert pianist at a grand piano, captured mid-performance with fingers gracefully touching the keys, their physical form beginning to dissolve into cascading waves of luminous musical notation and quantum energy. The transformation flows from fingertips upward: solid flesh transitions into flowing staves of glowing sheet music, floating notes, and brilliant light particles that spiral into the darkness above. Half of their body remains photorealistic with accurate human anatomy and emotional intensity, while the dissolving half transforms into fractal sound waves, bioluminescent musical symbols, and radiant plasma ribbons. Dramatic 3-point cinematic lighting: key light sculpting the pianist's face with deep shadows, fill light softly revealing the piano's polished surface, rim light creating Fresnel reflections along dissolving edges. The piano keys emit subtle volumetric light beams, illuminating floating dust and energy particles. Deep black background with atmospheric fog, strong vignette focusing attention on the transformation. Ultra-detailed: realistic skin texture and fabric on the remaining physical form, smooth gradients in the energy dissolution, reflective piano lacquer, 8K resolution, high contrast blue-orange color palette emphasizing the transcendent moment.

## 4. Arctic Fox in Blizzard
Professional wildlife photograph of a pristine white arctic fox standing alert on a snow-covered ridge during a fierce blizzard. Thick fur ripples in the wind, individual guard hairs visible and backlit creating a luminous halo effect. Dramatic 3-point lighting: pale blue key light from overcast sky illuminating the fox's face, soft fill light reflecting from snow preventing pure blacks, strong rim light creating pronounced Fresnel reflections along the fur edges separating the subject from the blizzard. Heavy volumetric fog and swirling snow particles create depth and atmosphere, with varying particle density and motion blur suggesting powerful wind. Sharp focus on the fox's piercing amber eyes and detailed facial features, shallow depth of field rendering background mountains as soft blue-gray silhouettes. Ultra-detailed 8K textures: individual fur strands, snow crystals caught in whiskers, subtle warm undertones in white fur, ice formations on rocks. Cool blue and white color palette with warm eye accent, cinematic vignette, professional nature photography composition, National Geographic quality.

## 5. Cyberpunk Noodle Cart
Hyperrealistic urban photograph of a futuristic street food vendor at a steaming noodle cart in a rain-soaked cyberpunk alley at night. The vendor ladles broth into bowls while holographic menus flicker overhead, neon signs reflect in puddles, and steam rises dramatically through shafts of colored light. Complex 3-point lighting: vibrant magenta neon key light from the left casting dramatic shadows, cyan fill light from storefront signs, and warm orange rim light from the cart's cooking flames creating Fresnel highlights on chrome surfaces and wet pavement. Volumetric fog and steam interact with multiple colored light sources, creating layered atmospheric depth. Rain droplets suspended mid-fall catch and refract the neon glow. Ultra-detailed 8K resolution: condensation on metal surfaces, intricate Asian characters on signs, realistic food textures (translucent noodles, glistening broth, fresh garnishes), worn vendor apron, reflective puddles. Shallow depth of field with sharp focus on the vendor's hands and the steaming bowl, bokeh from distant neon signs. High contrast blue-orange-magenta color palette, cinematic vignette, Blade Runner aesthetic, photorealistic materials.

Half the dancer remains photorealistic with perfect muscle definition and flowing costume fabric, while the dissolving portions create beautiful fractal patterns of organic matter and soft bioluminescent energy. Dramatic 3-point cinematic lighting: warm golden key light from upper left sculpting the dancer's face and torso, cool blue fill light from the right maintaining detail in shadows, rim light creating Fresnel edge glow where body meets leaves. Volumetric light rays pierce through the swirling leaves, revealing depth and movement. Deep gradient background transitioning from warm amber to cool twilight blue, strong vignette. Ultra-sharp 8K details: skin texture, fabric weave, individual leaf veins, smooth transformation gradients, atmospheric particles. Shallow DOF keeping the dancer's face razor-sharp while softly blurring outer leaf swirls. High contrast warm-cool color palette, poetic and ethereal mood.

Sharp focus on the motorcycle with shallow depth of field rendering the approaching storm as a dramatic but softer backdrop. High contrast blue-orange color palette (warm foreground against cool storm), cinematic vignette, professional automotive photography quality, apocalyptic mood with beautiful lighting.

Ultra-detailed 8K resolution: the molten glass's fluid surface tension, realistic subsurface scattering showing internal glow, sweat beads, worn leather gloves, soot-stained brick walls, tool textures. Shallow depth of field with razor-sharp focus on the glowing glass gather and the glassblower's concentrated expression. High contrast orange-blue color grading, deep shadows with preserved detail, cinematic vignette, Industrial Revolution aesthetic meets modern photographic techniques.

A ruby-throated hummingbird frozen mid-hover before a hibiscus flower, its form gracefully dissolving into flowing streams of liquid gemstones, crystalline structures, and radiant prismatic light. The transformation begins at the wingtips and tail: realistic feather detail and iridescent coloring transition into cascading rubies, emeralds, sapphires, and diamonds that flow like water, mixed with rainbow light refractions and glowing particle effects. Half the bird maintains photorealistic anatomy with accurate feather structure and the characteristic hovering pose, while dissolving portions create beautiful fluid jewelry patterns with bioluminescent energy. Dramatic 3-point lighting: warm golden key light from upper right creating highlights on remaining feathers and gem facets, soft green fill from foliage maintaining shadow detail, rim light producing strong Fresnel reflections and rainbow dispersion through the crystalline elements. Volumetric light shafts piercing through the transformation revealing suspended nectar droplets and pollen particles. Soft-focus garden background with bokeh flower shapes, strong vignette. Ultra-sharp 8K details: individual feather barbules, realistic gem facets with proper refraction, smooth feather-to-crystal transitions, flower petal texture, water droplets. Shallow DOF with razor-sharp focus on the bird's eye and head. Vibrant jewel-tone color palette with natural greens, magical and luminous mood.
