## Technical Notes for Qwen-Image Optimization

### Prompt Structure Elements Used:
1. **Subject Description**: Vivid, narrative-driven details for semantic understanding
2. **Technical Specifications**: Integrated camera, lens, and exposure naturally
3. **Lighting Details**: Specific sources, directions, and qualities for realism
4. **Color Information**: Grading, palettes, and moods to guide output
5. **Composition**: Perspectives, framing, and elements for balanced scenes
6. **Texture Details**: Emphasis on surfaces, materials, and fine elements
7. **Resolution**: Scaled K values based on detail needs
8. **Style Keywords**: Genre-specific terms for photographic authenticity
9. **Emotional Content**: Atmosphere and narrative to enhance engagement

### Why These Work for Qwen-Image:
- Relies on natural, descriptive language without syntax hacks
- Utilizes large context for layered, complex scene building
- Tests text handling in elements like signs or patterns
- Explores semantic grasp of diverse global and thematic motives
- Focuses on core model strengths without external aids
