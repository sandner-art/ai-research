## Technical Notes for Qwen-Image Optimization

### Prompt Structure Elements Used:
1. **Layout Description**: Detailed grids, columns, multi-page flows, and complex compositions
2. **Typography Specifications**: Varied fonts (serif, sans-serif, script, slab-serif, etc.), sizes, styles, and integrations
3. **Text Content**: Extensive texts, headlines, body copy, quotes, and labels for density
4. **3D Elements**: Extrusions, sculptures, holographics with perspectives and lighting
5. **Color Details**: Harmonies and applications for mood and emphasis
6. **Composition**: Advanced theory like grids, balance, hierarchy, and gestalt
7. **Texture and Effects**: Papers, metallics, distortions, and photorealistic renders
8. **Resolution**: Higher K values for intricate details
9. **Creative Tests**: Distortions, hybrids, and adherence to multiple font usages

### Why These Work for Qwen-Image:
- Exploits text rendering for complex, multi-font layouts and extensive copy
- Leverages large context for describing intricate pages and 3D structures
- Tests semantic grasp of design theory, font varieties, and layout complexity
- Encourages creative interpretations while maintaining prompt fidelity
- Natural language focus on photorealistic outputs without syntax tricks
